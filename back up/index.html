My On-the-Job Training (OJT) experience at the Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) SM Aura Branch was a valuable and enriching part of my academic journey as a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at STI College Global City. Throughout my internship, which lasted from February to June and consisted of 486 completed hours, I was given the opportunity to experience a professional work environment and apply the knowledge and skills I acquired during my studies. The internship allowed me to gain practical experience while developing both my technical and interpersonal skills.

During my training, I was assigned to the MS II department, where I assisted in ensuring the smooth operation of daily office activities. My responsibilities included providing operational support, assisting staff members with various tasks, and helping maintain the efficiency of office processes. Aside from supporting my assigned department, I was also given opportunities to assist other departments whenever technical assistance was needed. This exposure enabled me to gain a broader understanding of how different units within a government organization work together to deliver services to the public.

One of the most significant aspects of my internship was the opportunity to apply my technical knowledge in real workplace situations. As an Information Technology student, I was able to utilize concepts learned from my Network Technology and other IT-related subjects. I assisted in troubleshooting computer hardware and software issues, diagnosing network connectivity problems, and supporting basic network maintenance activities. I also gained hands-on experience with Ethernet cabling, crimping tools, and office computer systems. These responsibilities enhanced my confidence in performing technical tasks and improved my ability to solve problems efficiently.

In addition to technical work, I became familiar with several systems and applications used by Pag-IBIG Fund, including the Pag-IBIG ER Virtual platform, MP2 Savings Program processes, and Microsoft Excel. Learning how these systems operate gave me insight into how technology supports government services and improves operational efficiency. It also helped me appreciate the importance of accuracy, confidentiality, and professionalism when handling information and assisting with member-related transactions.

One of the challenges I encountered during my internship involved tasks related to the MP2 Savings Program. Since these transactions involve sensitive and confidential information, it was important to exercise caution and responsibility when performing assigned duties. This experience taught me the importance of attention to detail and following established procedures to ensure that information remains secure and accurate. By remaining focused, observing workplace protocols, and seeking guidance from supervisors when necessary, I was able to perform my tasks effectively while maintaining professionalism.

Beyond the technical skills I developed, the internship also strengthened my communication, teamwork, and adaptability. Working in a professional environment required me to interact with employees from different departments, follow organizational policies, and adjust to various work situations. These experiences helped me become more confident in collaborating with others and responding to workplace challenges. I learned that success in the workplace depends not only on technical competence but also on having a positive attitude, strong work ethic, and willingness to learn.

Perhaps the most important lesson I learned during my OJT experience was the value of consistency. Performing tasks accurately, responsibly, and consistently contributes to the overall success of an organization. I realized that even small contributions can make a significant difference in maintaining efficient operations and providing quality public service. This lesson will continue to guide me as I pursue my future career in the field of information technology.

Overall, my OJT experience at Pag-IBIG Fund SM Aura Branch was both educational and rewarding. It provided me with practical knowledge, workplace exposure, and professional skills that cannot be fully acquired inside a classroom. The experience strengthened my technical capabilities, improved my understanding of office operations, and prepared me for the expectations of the professional world. Through this internship, I gained valuable insights that will help me become a more competent, responsible, and effective IT professional in the future.
